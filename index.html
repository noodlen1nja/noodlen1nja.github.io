01.About

I'm a Senior ML/AI Engineer at Creditsafe in Dublin, where I lead the design and delivery of production LLM-powered agentic AI systems on AWS, using Bedrock AgentCore as the runtime and the Strands Agents framework for multi-agent orchestration.

Before agents, my work centred on the systems that keep ML honest in production: MLOps automation, a recommendation service running across multiple countries, and evaluation and governance frameworks that turn non-deterministic models into something you can confidently gate a deployment on.

That sits on 8+ years of software engineering, from Angular and GraphQL banking applications to Python automation and analytics, plus an MSc in Computing (Data Analytics) from Dublin City University. I hold the AWS Certified Machine Learning – Specialty and Solutions Architect – Associate certifications.

  • Agentic AI

    Multi-agent systems using the agents-as-tools pattern on Bedrock AgentCore.

  • MLOps

    Automated training, evaluation and conditional deployment pipelines.

  • Evaluation & governance

    Regression, ranking and top-K metrics with full model lineage.

  • Recommender systems

    Content-based and collaborative filtering served from SageMaker.
